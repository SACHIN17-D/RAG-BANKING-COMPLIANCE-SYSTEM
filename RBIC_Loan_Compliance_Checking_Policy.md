## SECTION 1: BORROWER STATUTORY ELIGIBILITY & CRILC VERIFICATION

### 1.1 Legal Entity Identifier (LEI) Mandate
- All corporate borrowers with aggregate credit exposure of **INR 50 Crores ($10M) and above** from the banking system must obtain and maintain an active Legal Entity Identifier (LEI) code prior to sanction, renewal, or enhancement of credit facilities.
- Facilities shall not be disbursed to corporate entities without an active, verified LEI.

### 1.2 CRILC (Central Repository of Information on Large Credits) Verification
- Mandatory pre-sanction screening of the CRILC database for all aggregate exposures of **INR 5 Crores and above**.
- Assessment must verify total banking system exposure, current Special Mention Account (SMA) status across all member banks, and non-performing debt history.

### 1.3 Willful Defaulters & Non-Cooperative Borrowers
- **Zero Credit Exposure** is permitted to entities, directors, or promoters appearing on the Reserve Bank / CIBIL willful defaulters or suit-filed lists.
- No compromise settlement or restructuring can be granted to willful defaulters without clearance from the Special High-Powered Board Committee.

### 1.4 KYC, AML & Ultimate Beneficial Ownership (UBO)
- Mandatory identification and verification of Ultimate Beneficial Owners (UBO) holding **10% or more** controlling equity interest or voting power.
- Annual AML screening against United Nations Security Council (UNSC) sanctions and domestic financial intelligence watchlists.

### 1.5 Statutory Credit Restrictions (Banking Regulation Act Section 20)
- Strictly prohibited from granting loans against the security of the bank's own shares.
- Strictly prohibited from advancing credit facilities to Bank Directors, senior executives, or their relatives without explicit prior approval by the Board of Directors / Management Credit Committee.

### 1.6 Early Warning Red-Flag Triggers (Mandatory Escalation within 48 Hours)
- Unhedged foreign currency exposure exceeding **25%** of the borrower's tangible net worth.
- Frequent resignation of statutory auditors or abrupt changes in accounting policies in the preceding 24 months.
- Routing of funds through non-consortium bank accounts or unverified transactions with sister concerns.

## SECTION 2: MANDATORY FINANCIAL BENCHMARK RATIOS & UNDERWRITING

### 2.1 Debt Service Coverage Ratio (DSCR)
- **Minimum Average DSCR:** Over the term loan repayment tenure must not be less than **1.50x**.
- **Stressed Sensitivity Analysis:** In any individual operational projection year, the DSCR must not fall below **1.25x** under a 10% revenue drop or 200 bps interest rate hike stress test.

### 2.2 Current Ratio & Working Capital Liquidity
- **Minimum Benchmark:** Established at **1.33x** for operating Cash Credit (CC) and Overdraft (OD) working capital facilities.
- Borrowers with current ratio between 1.20x and 1.33x require mandatory Credit Risk Mitigation approvals and higher collateral margins.

### 2.3 Total Outside Liabilities to Tangible Net Worth (TOL / TNW Leverage)
- **Manufacturing Sector:** Maximum allowable TOL/TNW is capped at **3.50x**.
- **Trading & Services Sector:** Maximum allowable TOL/TNW is capped at **4.00x**.
- **Infrastructure Projects:** Maximum allowable TOL/TNW is capped at **5.00x** during the construction and implementation phase.

### 2.4 Interest Coverage Ratio (ICR)
- Operating EBITDA divided by Gross Interest Expense must maintain a minimum benchmark of **2.00x** across all quarterly compliance reviews.

### 2.5 Promoters Equity Contribution & Margin Norms
- Minimum Promoters' unencumbered equity contribution is **25%** of the total estimated project cost.
- Upfront equity infusion verified by a statutory auditor certificate is required prior to the first debt drawdown.

### 2.6 Working Capital Assessment Methodologies
- **Nayak Committee Turnover Method:** Applicable for credit limits up to INR 5 Crores (20% of projected annual gross turnover as bank finance, 5% promoter margin).
- **Cash Budget Method:** Mandatory for seasonal industries (sugar, tea), infrastructure contractors, and software export units.
- **Tandon Committee MPBF (Method II):** Mandatory for large manufacturing credits exceeding INR 10 Crores.

## SECTION 3: LOAN-TO-VALUE (LTV) LIMITS & COLLATERAL SECURITY NORMS

### 3.1 Commercial Real Estate (CRE) LTV & Valuation
- Maximum Loan-to-Value (LTV) for Commercial Real Estate loans (commercial complexes, IT parks, retail malls) is strictly capped at **75%** of realizable market value.
- Independent valuation reports from **two registered valuers** are mandatory for exposures exceeding INR 10 Crores.

### 3.2 Individual Housing Loan LTV Framework & Risk Weights
- **Tier 1 (Loans up to INR 30 Lakhs / $40k):** Maximum LTV = **90%** (Risk Weight: 35%).
- **Tier 2 (Loans above INR 30 Lakhs to 75 Lakhs):** Maximum LTV = **80%** (Risk Weight: 50%).
- **Tier 3 (Loans above INR 75 Lakhs):** Maximum LTV = **75%** (Risk Weight: 75%).

### 3.3 Mandatory CERSAI Registration
- All equitable mortgages, registered mortgages, and hypothecations on immovable and movable assets must be registered with **CERSAI** (Central Registry of Securitisation Asset Reconstruction and Security Interest) within **30 days** of creation.
- Non-compliance within 30 days results in mandatory internal audit classification as a **Severe Documentation Defect**.

### 3.4 Title Search Reports (TSR) & Legal Due Diligence
- A comprehensive Title Search Report (TSR) verifying non-encumbrance and a continuous **30-year chain of title** must be obtained from an empanelled advocate.

### 3.5 Insurance Coverage & Stock Audits
- **100% Comprehensive Insurance:** Mandatory coverage for fire, earthquake, flood, and transit with a Bank Hypothecation and Mortgage Clause.
- **Stock & Book Debt Audits:** Annual physical audit by an independent empanelled Chartered Accountant firm is mandatory for credit limits of **INR 5 Crores and above**.

## SECTION 4: PRUDENTIAL ASSET CLASSIFICATION & PROVISIONING NORMS (IRAC)

### 4.1 Special Mention Account (SMA) Early Warning Sub-Categories
- **SMA-0:** Principal or interest payment overdue between **1 to 30 days**.
- **SMA-1:** Principal or interest payment overdue between **31 to 60 days** (Escalate to Joint Lenders Forum / corrective action plan).
- **SMA-2:** Principal or interest payment overdue between **61 to 90 days** (Mandatory CRILC reporting and immediate credit review).

### 4.2 Non-Performing Asset (NPA) Classification Criteria
- **Term Loans:** Interest or principal instalment remains overdue for more than **90 days**.
- **Cash Credit / Overdraft:** Account remains "Out of Order" (outstanding balance continuously exceeds the sanctioned limit/drawing power) for more than **90 days**.
- **Agricultural Loans:** Unpaid for 2 crop seasons (short-duration crops) or 1 crop season (long-duration crops).

### 4.3 Prudential Provisioning Rates on Loan Assets
- **Standard Assets (General Commercial):** **0.40%** provisioning on total funded outstanding.
- **Standard Assets (Commercial Real Estate - CRE):** **1.00%** provisioning on funded outstanding.
- **Standard Assets (Direct Agriculture & SME Units):** **0.25%** provisioning.
- **Substandard Assets (NPA up to 12 months):** **15%** provision on secured portion; **25%** on unsecured portion.
- **Doubtful-1 (D1, NPA 1-2 years):** **25%** secured provision + **100%** unsecured provision.
- **Doubtful-2 (D2, NPA 2-3 years):** **40%** secured provision + **100%** unsecured provision.
- **Doubtful-3 (D3, NPA > 3 years):** **100%** provision on total outstanding exposure.
- **Loss Assets:** **100%** complete write-off or provision immediately upon identification.

## SECTION 5: LARGE EXPOSURE FRAMEWORK (LEF) & CONCENTRATION LIMITS

### 5.1 Single Counterparty Exposure Ceiling
- Total aggregate exposure (funded + non-funded) to a single corporate entity cannot exceed **20%** of the Bank's Eligible Tier 1 Capital base.
- Under exceptional circumstances, the Board of Directors may approve an additional 5% exposure (up to **25% maximum**).

### 5.2 Connected Group Counterparties Limit
- Total exposure to a group of connected counterparties is strictly capped at **25%** of the Bank's Eligible Tier 1 Capital.

### 5.3 Sectoral & Capital Market Exposure (CME) Ceilings
- **Capital Market Exposure (CME):** Aggregate advances against shares, IPO financing, and equity underwriting capped at **40%** of Bank Net Worth.
- **Real Estate Sectoral Cap:** Aggregate commercial real estate lending capped at **15%** of the total bank credit portfolio.

### 5.4 Unhedged Foreign Currency Exposure (UFCE) Surcharge
- Borrowers with unhedged FX exposures face mandatory incremental capital risk weights (**20% to 100%**) and incremental provisioning of **10 to 40 basis points** based on projected earnings volatility.

### 5.5 Priority Sector Lending (PSL) Targets
- Mandatory **40%** of Adjusted Net Bank Credit (ANBC) allocated to Agriculture, Micro, Small & Medium Enterprises (MSME), Export Credit, and Affordable Housing.

## SECTION 6: ESG DUE DILIGENCE, DISBURSEMENT & AUDIT CHECKLIST

### 6.1 Environmental, Social & Governance (ESG) Due Diligence
- Phase 1 Environmental Site Assessment (ESA) mandatory for industrial projects exceeding **INR 25 Crores**.
- Mandatory verification of valid Consent to Establish (CTE) and Consent to Operate (CTO) from the State Pollution Control Board before loan release.

### 6.2 End-Use of Funds Monitoring & Anti-Diversion Safeguards
- **Direct Disbursement:** Disbursed directly into verified supplier or contractor accounts; direct cash disbursements or transfers to group affiliates are strictly barred.
- **CA End-Use Certificate:** Quarterly CA certificate required for all working capital credit lines exceeding **INR 1 Crore**.

### 6.3 Pre-Disbursement Compliance Checklist (Mandatory Sign-Offs)
- [X] **1. Sanction Letter:** Formal acceptance of loan terms and restrictive covenants by authorized signatories.
- [X] **2. Guarantees:** Personal and corporate guarantees executed along with CA net worth certificates.
- [X] **3. ROC Charge Registration:** First charge registered with Registrar of Companies (Form CHG-1) within 30 days.
- [X] **4. CERSAI Filing:** Unique security interest identifier generated and recorded.
- [X] **5. Insurance Assignment:** Comprehensive insurance policy assigned in favor of the lending bank.
- [X] **6. Title Search Report:** 30-year non-encumbrance title search report confirmed by panel legal counsel.
